Measure, compare and configure your public inbox, outbox, DMs, search and private storage.

01 — Identity

An npub is read-only. Prefer an extension for signing. An nsec stays in application memory; never enter one on an untrusted deployment.

02 — Discover & measure

Private storage URLs are never discovered automatically or included in public relay lists. Listing a relay here confirms your trust, not its privacy. Confirm that its operator enforces access control. These URLs stay in this tab and are encrypted to you when saved.

Recommendations use separate evidence for each role. Inbox: mentions queries. Outbox: visible public notes. DM: declared NIP-17/59 support and a recipient query, or your explicit confirmation of gift-wrap storage support. Search: declared NIP-50 and matching results. Empty queries do not prove storage or write access. Paid/restricted relays need your access confirmation; no purchases or account creation happen here.

03 — Compare & choose what changes

Only checked roles change. Public inbox = where others send mentions; outbox = where others find your posts. Your client's feed relay settings may be separate. Client support for DM, search and private lists varies.

04 — Archive, publish & roll back

Backups restore the configuration kinds they contain; a newly created list that was absent from a backup is not automatically removed. Backups contain signed configuration events, including encrypted private lists, and no secret keys. Keep your original backup somewhere safe.

Publishing first downloads the original archive and all signed updates. Each list publishes separately, so partial success is possible. Rollback imports the original signed backup and re-signs its contents with a newer timestamp. Importing a backup alone makes no network changes.
